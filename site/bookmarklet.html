Isolated Sources

Bookmarklet

This runs on other websites. Isolated Sources' security policy and airplane-mode self-test do not apply there. It does not send the text to a server.

Drag the link to your bookmarks bar. On another page, select text and open the bookmark. It reads that selection and hands it to a tab on this site. The text is scrubbed in that tab. It is not uploaded.
